THE PROCESS

The creative process
isn’t linear.

In your head, every idea is a perfectly straight line. Then the real work starts: you loop back, lose the thread, hit a low, flip the whole thing upside down and climb out again. That mess is not a sign you are failing. It is the process. Keep moving through it and you will reach done, and then the next idea.
