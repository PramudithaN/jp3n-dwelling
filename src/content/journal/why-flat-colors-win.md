## The Monoculture of Synthetic Gradients

For the past five years, tech agency templates have relied on a predictable crutch: purple-to-blue linear gradients, blurred glassmorphic cards, and floating 3D blobs.

When every digital product uses the same blurred gradients, brand distinctiveness collapses to zero.

## Structural Color Theory

In physical building, timber, stone, and brass do not blend into translucent jelly. They hold their edges.

By committing strictly to flat, verified color tokens (#7B542F timber, #B6771D ochre, #FF9D00 illumination amber, and #17100A espresso), interfaces gain tactile presence and unmistakable brand clarity.
