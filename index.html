Atelier Principles

Three tenets merci. lives by.

01

The textile dictates the cut.

We do not force rigid trends onto delicate cloth. The natural drape, grain, and breathability of raw wool and washed linen guide every seam.

02

Impermanence of trend, permanence of structure.

Garments built to last thirty years outlive momentary micro-trends. Double-faced construction and hand-bound interior hems ensure enduring dignity.

03

Garments as personal architecture.

A coat is portable shelter. It provides warmth, tactile calm, and effortless poise the moment you step into the world.

Atelier Origins

We design garments that reveal their luxury through touch, not loud logos.

Founded between Kyoto's historic textile quarters and Milan's sartorial workshops, merci. was born from a desire for tactile honesty. In a world saturated with synthetic disposables, we slow down.

Every pattern is drafted by hand on heavy kraft paper. We partner with multi-generational family mills that spin raw organic wool, unbleached flax, and wild mulberry silk.

When you wear a piece from merci., you feel the weight of intention. It moves with your body, softens with every wear, and becomes a permanent companion.

The Craft Cycle

From raw fiber to your silhouette.

  1. 01

    Fiber Selection & Sourcing

    We source long-staple virgin merino wool, Belgian flax, and raw mulberry silk directly from certified ethical farms with unblemished provenance.

  2. 02

    Drafting & Three-Dimensional Drape

    Patterns are draped in unbleached calico muslin on live forms, tested in dynamic motion, and refined for natural ease of movement.

  3. 03

    Precision Cutting & Hand Tailoring

    Garments are individually bench-cut. Master tailors construct inner canvas chest pieces, horn buttonholes, and hand-bound french seams.

  4. 04

    Numbered Labeling & Archiving

    Every edition is hand-stamped with its unique batch number, certified for lifetime repair, and dispatched in recyclable linen garment bags.

Inquiries

Before you acquire.

Our silhouettes are tailored with relaxed architectural ease. If you prefer a crisp closer fit, choose your usual standard size; if you love layered volume, size up one step. We provide complimentary sleeve and hem adjustments upon checkout.

Pure wools and silks have natural antimicrobial and self-cleaning attributes. Airing your garments outdoors after wearing is usually sufficient. When necessary, we recommend gentle green eco dry-cleaning or cold hand wash with cedar soap.

Yes. Our master atelier accepts a limited allotment of bespoke appointments each season. We will craft an individual pattern based on your measurements and chosen archive cloth.

All garments are shipped complimentary worldwide via carbon-neutral express courier. We provide a lifetime repair guarantee: if a seam tears or a button loosens at any point in the garment's life, return it to our atelier for restorative hand-repair.
